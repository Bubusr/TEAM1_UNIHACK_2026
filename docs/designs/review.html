Visual direction: meadow journal

The proposed direction is intentionally tactile and slightly imperfect: a field notebook from a small animal sanctuary, not a generic fintech dashboard. Use texture sparingly behind content, keep controls legible, and let real animal photography carry the emotional weight.

ink green terracotta paper sage harvest gold
  • Use one expressive serif for titles and a quiet sans-serif for utility text.
  • Prefer irregular paper edges, hand-drawn strokes, and layered illustrations over glass cards.
  • Use asymmetry to create a path through the farm, but preserve predictable tap targets.
  • Reserve rounded panels for real containers: shop drawer, pet details, consent, and results.
  • Show the farm as a place first; resource counters should be secondary and compact.
  • Use real captured pet images in frames with simple crop/print treatments, not emoji as the primary identity.

Use-case review

These are the states that should be approved before implementation. Select a decision and add a note for anything that feels wrong.

First launchExplain camera, animal welfare, location, and notification permissions before asking the OS prompt.
Camera deniedShow a calm explanation and settings path; never leave the user on an endless loading state.
Offline captureSave a clearly labelled pending job, show retry status, and prevent duplicate rewards after reconnect.
Unsafe photoPerson face, private property, animal distress, or chasing is rejected with a welfare explanation.
Returning animalShow “Mochi again” and EXP instead of pretending a new pet was created.
Garden at a glanceFarm landmarks remain the focus; fence timer, Kibble, and navigation do not cover the scene.
Care cap reachedExplain when feed, pet, or play is unavailable and show the next reset time.
Crop lifecycleEmpty plot, seed planted, watered, growing, harvest-ready, and no-seed states each need distinct visuals.
Shop purchaseShop is a separate drawer/modal; show cost, balance, insufficient funds, and purchase confirmation.
Fence vulnerableUse an observable visual change and a gentle warning; never use panic language for a game timer.
Mint pending/failsPet remains usable off-chain, with a quiet pending badge and a retry path if the chain fails.
Lure and rescueUse consent-like confirmation, owner grace period, transparent odds, cost cap, and guaranteed recovery.
Consensus voterBlurred image, no requester identity, timer, accessible Approve/Report controls, and a reason on report.
QR expired/reusedExplain expiry or prior redemption and never silently grant a shield or discount.
Shelter impactShow sponsor contribution, fulfilment status, and what is confirmed versus still pending.
