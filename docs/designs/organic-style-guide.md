# Organic visual direction: Meadow Journal

## Why the first direction missed

The original review board used repeated dark cards, identical rounded
containers, high-saturation accent counters, and evenly spaced dashboard
sections. That pattern communicates a generic product admin screen rather than
a living farm with animals and care routines.

## Proposed direction

**Meadow Journal** treats the product like a field notebook from a small
animal sanctuary:

- Warm paper background with restrained fiber/noise texture.
- Sage, ink green, terracotta, cream, and harvest gold.
- Expressive serif display type paired with a highly readable sans-serif.
- Irregular paper frames, hand-drawn outlines, layered crops, and imperfect
  alignment.
- Real pet photographs used as the emotional focal point.
- UI controls stay quiet and rectangular enough to remain accessible.
- The farm scene is the hero; counters and status information support it.

## Reference research

These are references for principles and mood, not assets to copy:

- [Organic web design examples](https://99designs.com/inspiration/websites/organic):
  earthy palettes, natural imagery, and editorial composition.
- [Natural textures in web design](https://silphiumdesign.com/what-are-natural-textures-in-web-design/):
  restrained paper, linen, wood, watercolor, and stone textures.
- [Asymmetrical layouts influenced by nature](https://silphiumdesign.com/guide-to-asymmetrical-layouts-influenced-nature/):
  offset composition and movement without losing hierarchy.
- [Willowbrook cozy game UI](https://breathinghuman.itch.io/willowbrook-ui):
  reference for tactile panels, farm inventory, shops, and dialogue.
- [Cozy farming UI kit](https://swarmpulsestudio.itch.io/cozy-farming-sim-ui-kit-170-text-free-panels-icons-buttons-and-trackers):
  reference for quiet resource panels and readable farming controls.
- [King's Lynn Leisure Farm on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4056300/Kings_Lynn_Leisure_Farm/):
  reference for cheerful farm composition with barns, ponds, pens, paths,
  decorations, and many animal areas.

## Guardrails against “AI slop”

1. Do not make every section a floating rounded rectangle.
2. Do not use gradients, neon purple, glassmorphism, or generic dashboard
   metric tiles.
3. Do not put a resource counter above the farm's main visual hierarchy.
4. Do not use emoji as the final pet art when a captured image is available.
5. Every decorative mark must support place, care, season, or animal identity.
6. Every empty, loading, denied, offline, and failed state gets a designed
   response rather than a spinner.
7. Prefer one strong illustration or photo over many small decorative icons.
