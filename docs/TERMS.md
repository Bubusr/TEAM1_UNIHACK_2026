# PawVerse terms baseline

PawVerse is a prototype. Rewards, pets, and future blockchain assets are experimental and may change or be removed before launch.

Users must only capture animals they are permitted to photograph and must not submit people, private documents, screens, stock images, or harmful content. The service may reject content, suspend accounts, and remove content that violates safety or animal-welfare rules.

Production terms, age gate, promotions rules, wallet disclosures, and account deletion instructions require legal review before real users are invited.
