# MĂNG NON (Knowledge Grove) — Core Lore, Gameplay & Art Direction

*(Creative Design Document — companion to technical specification "Idea03_MangNon_Tonghop.md". This document establishes what this world is, how it feels to inhabit it, and its visual identity.)*

---

## 0. Logline

> One quiet night in an ancient bamboo grove, you discover a small glowing creature waiting to learn about the world — where every page you study, line of code you write, and quiet hour you focus gently breathes life into a shared tree of knowledge, blossoming into tangible educational scholarships and student support.

---

## 1. The World

### 1.1 What is this realm?
Not ancient Vietnam, nor any specific marked coordinates on a map — but a **sanctuary suspended between memory, folklore, and a quiet study desk (Cozy Study Sanctuary)**. The realm echoes with fragments of two beloved folk tales:
- **Thánh Gióng (Saint Gióng):** Where an ordinary child grows through the rice and nourishment offered freely by the whole village, and once the harvest of peace is won, ascends to the sky leaving behind enduring footprints for those who follow.
- **The Tale of Princess Kaguya:** A magical spirit cradled inside a luminous stalk of bamboo, found unexpectedly in the dark.

Here, the ancient bamboo grove births small beings called **Măng Non** (Green Bamboo Shoots). They are not born to wage wars or fulfill grand destinies. They exist simply to **grow alongside a human being who is learning, reading, and living**. In an age saturated with digital distraction, burnout, and intrusive surveillance, Măng Non is an emotional anchor — a quiet friend sitting right beside your desk.

### 1.2 The Ancient Bamboo Grove — Personal Study Sanctuary
- A bamboo forest that feels boundless — bamboo here is never felled; stalks grow for a thousand years, aging into a deep amber-gold patina.
- **Cozy Study Corner:** The Grove represents your personal learning sanctuary. A weathered wooden table, a warm amber lamp, and Măng Non resting quietly beside your books or laptop.
- **True Real-World Day/Night Sync:** Synced to your local clock. Daytime filters soft volumetric light through bamboo canopies; at dusk, fireflies stir, creating the tender ambiance where glowing bamboo stalks are found.
- **Learning Seasons:**
  - *Spring (Sprout Season):* Fresh beginnings, setting intentions for new courses and habits.
  - *Summer (Lush Canopy):* Deep focus through midterms, big projects, and intensive study marathons.
  - *Autumn (Whispering Leaves):* Slower contemplation, steady revision.
  - *Winter (Resting Roots):* Warm evenings with tea, reflection, and quiet presence.
- **Intimate and Private:** Your grove belongs solely to you and your companion. No strangers, no leaderboards, no unsolicited interruptions.

### 1.3 The Glowing Bamboo Stalk
- Inspired by the luminous bamboo of Kaguya lore, but here each glowing stalk opens **only once for one unique companion**. When cracked open, the golden warmth dissolves softly into the night, and the stalk returns to peaceful wood.
- **Visuals:** Warm amber glow radiating from bamboo nodes, with tiny floating particles like fireflies. This is the **most tender animation sequence** in the entire experience.
- **Narrative Meaning:** The grove does not gift a generic pre-made avatar. It simply opens a doorway. The living creature inside is born from what your own hands draw, photograph, or upload. The grove provides the spark; you bestow life.

### 1.4 Phù Đổng Village & The Sacred Tree of Knowledge
- A tranquil village nestled at the edge of the bamboo grove, representing the **physical embodiment of all learners and students**: every thatched roof and soft lamp represents another person studying somewhere in the world tonight.
- In the village square stands **The Sacred Banyan Tree (Cây Tri Thức)** — its ancient roots plunge deep into the soil. The tree stays vibrant through the quiet focus and dedication of all scholars in the village.
- **The Phù Đổng Student Scholarship Pool:** Whenever students complete focus sessions and gift leaves to the tree, their nỗ lực is recorded on-chain into a public goods scholarship pool. Partner bookstores (Fahasa), educational platforms, and local study cafes match this energy to fund real-world books and scholarships for underprivileged students.
- **The Blossom Event:** When the tree reaches community milestones, it triggers a breathtaking bloom of fiery red rice flowers (*hoa gạo*) or a sky filled with fireflies — blessing every active companion in the village.

### 1.5 The Stream of Memories
- A crystal-clear brook meandering between the private grove and the village.
- When a companion finishes an academic semester, a major project, or a reading journey, it partakes in the **Resting Ritual**. It transforms into a **smooth river pebble resting on the stream bed**, forever retaining its silhouette and memories as a Soulbound NFT on Avalanche Fuji. Water glides over it endlessly, but the stone of memory never fades.

## 2. The Companion (Măng Non)

### 2.1 Who it is, and who it is not
- **Not a pet to collect** (no rarity tiers, no trading for speculative profit).
- **Not an all-knowing assistant or tutor** — it remains **more innocent than the learner**, embodying the joy of discovering the world together.
- **A genuine companion of reflection** — its understanding of life is shaped entirely by what you choose to share with it. Two companions nurtured by two different learners will perceive the world uniquely.

### 2.2 Cognitive Growth (Replacing Level and XP)
Rather than abstract numerical XP, Măng Non develops through **4 natural cognitive stages**:
1. **Raw Curiosity (Newborn):** Asks innocent, fundamental questions (*"Why does day turn into night?"*).
2. **Association:** Begins weaving threads between stories you previously told (*"Last week you spoke of autumn leaves, and today you mentioned winter exams — do they connect?"*).
3. **Personal Perspective:** Expresses gentle opinions, soft aesthetic preferences, and playful banter.
4. **Deep Companionship:** Offers quiet comfort, emotional grounding, and understanding during stressful exam weeks.

### 2.3 Personalities and Voice Samples
| Trait | Dialogue Dynamics | Greeting Sample |
|---|---|---|
| **Curious** | Asks questions with genuine wonder, loves details | *"Did you just discover something new? Tell me more! What did it look like?"* |
| **Tranquil** | Quiet, pauses comfortably, values silence | *"...Ah. So that is what you learned today. I will hold that in my heart."* |
| **Playful** | Cheerful, loves gentle guessing games, smiles easily | *"Oh wonderful!! Let me guess — does that connect to what you worked on yesterday?"* |
| **Shy** | Soft-spoken, hesitates gently, opens up as you talk | *"...You came back. I... I was thinking about what you told me earlier."* |

### 2.4 Visual Identity — "Keep the Original, Breathe Life Into It"
- When a user sketches by hand, takes a photo, or uploads an image, the system **never redraws or generative-replaces it**.
- The algorithm cuts out the background, generates a crisp handcrafted paper sticker border, articulates 16 anatomical joints, and applies gentle organic breathing deformation (like the animated paper puppet Teri in *The Amazing World of Gumball*).
- The original hand-drawn imperfections remain intact, because your own drawing is the heart of the relationship.

### 2.5 Focus Sessions & "Proof of Output" (Show-and-Tell)
- **Absolute Silence During Study (Silence Mode):** When starting a 15, 25, or 45-minute focus session, the interface turns quiet. No alarming notifications, no intrusive pop-ups, no tracking of browser tabs. You are free to read textbooks, write code, or draft notes in deep flow.
- **Zero Surveillance & No Surprise Snaps:** The app strictly forbids unannounced webcam snaps or continuous recording. Surveillance creates anxiety; trust fosters deep focus.
- **Show-and-Tell at Session End:** When the timer finishes, you can optionally snap a picture of your freshly written notebook page, math proof, or code terminal to show your companion.
- **Măng Non's Reaction:** It looks upon your work with curiosity: *"You wrote so many fresh ink lines today, what does this theorem mean?"* or *"These lines of code look like magic runes, what do they build?"*. Your focus transforms into Mam Luc, gifting a green leaf to the community tree.

## 3. Narrative Arc

**Act 1 — Discovery (Onboarding, first 3 minutes):**
A dark bamboo grove, a glowing stalk, sketching your first companion, and connecting your student email (`.edu.vn`) for quiet protection. Mood: quiet wonder, gentleness.

**Act 2 — Growing Together (Core Loop through the semester):**
No rigid checklists — open the app whenever you sit at your desk or visit a partner study cafe. Măng Non sits quietly beside you during silent focus sessions, listening to what you studied. Mood: steady, comforting, grounded.

**Act 3 — Resting & Graduation (Journey Completion):**
When a semester ends or a major thesis is defended, Măng Non doesn't vanish. It holds a tender farewell ritual, thanks you in its own voice, becomes a river stone in the Stream of Memories, and imprints an immutable JourneyNFT onto Avalanche Fuji. Its accrued Mam Luc feeds the Student Scholarship Pool to aid another student in need. Mood: gentle pride, nostalgic warmth, no sorrow.

**The Seasonal Cycle:**
When the Sacred Tree bursts into crimson blossom, you know your quiet hours have joined thousands of hours from students across the country, keeping the lamp of learning burning bright.

## 4. Emotional Core

**Core Message:** *"Growing up does not happen because someone threatened you with a timer, but because someone is waiting quietly to hear what you learned."*

Every design decision must answer one question:  
**"Does this make the student feel warmly awaited, or coldly pressured?"**  
If it feels like pressure, redesign it.

**Three Guiding Words:** Gentle — Intimate — Non-Judgmental.

## 5. Art Style

### 5.1 Two Aesthetic Pillars
**A. Atmospheric Depth inspired by Studio Ghibli:**
Used for the **environment and background**: volumetric moonlight through bamboo leaves, shifting weather, morning mists, and the tranquil glow of a paper lantern.

**B. Bold Flat Linework inspired by Đông Hồ Folk Woodblock Prints:**
Used for the **UI, icons, ink palettes, and symbolic artifacts**: handcrafted bamboo slips, woodblock stamps, and organic paper textures.

### 5.2 Color Palette
- **Deep Forest Greens:** `#1B2A2F` (Night Abyss), `#2D4A3E` (Deep Bamboo), `#3E5C45` (Soft Bamboo).
- **Warm Amber Highlights:** `#F4A259` (Warm Amber), `#FFD166` (Warm Glow) — the primary focal point in every screen.
- **Đông Hồ Crimson Accents:** `#E8837A` (Rice Flower Bloom), `#B23B3B` (Woodblock Red).
- **Dó Paper Tones:** `#F6EBD4` (Aged Dó Paper), `#EDE0C8` (Muted Parchment).
